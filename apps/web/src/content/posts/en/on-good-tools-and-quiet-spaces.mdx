A room with a door is worth more than a room with a better chair. The
cheapest performance improvement available to most people is not a tool
at all — it is fifteen uninterrupted minutes.

## Tools should disappear

The best tool is the one you stop noticing. If you are thinking about
your editor, you are not thinking about the problem.

That is the whole test:

- Does it stay out of the way when nothing is wrong?
- Does it tell you clearly when something is?
- Can you still read what you wrote two years ago?

Anything that fails the third question is not a tool. It is a lease.

## Space is a tool

Desks, like editors, are mostly about what they let you ignore. A clean
surface is not an aesthetic preference; it is a way of reducing the
number of things competing to be remembered.

The quiet room and the plain text file are the same idea in two
materials.
